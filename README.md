# Yijun Li

**Biostatistics · AI · Spatial Biology**  
Research Fellow, Department of Data Science · Dana-Farber Cancer Institute

[Website](https://codingpoppy.github.io/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=I3OCMVwAAAAJ&hl=en) · [CV](https://codingpoppy.github.io/assets/CV_Yijun_Li.pdf) · [Email](mailto:liyijun@ds.dfci.harvard.edu)

I develop statistical and deep learning methods to understand **how biological organization varies across individuals and relates to disease**. My work connects biological networks, spatial tissue architecture, and tumor immune phenotypes through interpretable models of complex biomedical data.

## Research directions

- **Biological networks across individuals.** BSNMani, its scalable extension BSNMani+, and SPIN connect variation in brain and molecular networks to clinical outcomes. [Explore this work →](https://codingpoppy.github.io/research.html#biological-networks)
- **Multiscale tissue architecture.** CTMap learns interpretable anatomical regions from the spatial organization of cell types. *Manuscript in submission.* [Research overview →](https://codingpoppy.github.io/research.html#tissue-architecture)
- **Immune profiling from histopathology.** HistoProfile uses weakly supervised deep learning to estimate immune biomarkers from routine H&E slides. [Preprint →](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.06.757083v1)
